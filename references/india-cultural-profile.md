# India Cultural Profile

Load when scoring "Cultural fit". Use as a checklist of hypotheses to test,
not as fixed rules. India is many markets; never treat these as universal.

## Principles

1. **Region before country.** North, south, east, west and each state differ in
   language, food, dress, festivals and price expectations.
2. **Urban vs small-town.** Metros often accept English-first design. Tier-2/3
   towns often respond better to local language and simpler flows.
3. **Trust is earned visibly.** Real address, real phone, named people, reviews
   and clear policies matter more than polished slogans.
4. **Family decisions.** Many purchases are discussed with family. Content
   that helps someone explain the choice to others (comparisons, FAQs,
   shareable summaries) tends to help.
5. **Value consciousness.** Show total price, EMI options, offers and
   what is included. Hidden charges damage trust.

## Seasonal timing

Festival dates change every year and differ by region and calendar.
**Verify the current year's dates before planning content.** Typical anchors:
Diwali, Holi, Eid, Navratri and Durga Puja, Pongal and Onam (south),
Ganesh Chaturthi, wedding season, school admissions and exam periods, and
large e-commerce sale events. Publish and optimise well before the event, since
search interest builds ahead of it.

## Copy and design checks

| Check | Why |
|-------|-----|
| Tone matches the target region and age group | Over-formal or over-casual copy reads wrong |
| No stereotyped imagery or one-size-fits-all "Indian" visuals | Diversity across regions |
| Photos and testimonials reflect the target region's people | Relatability |
| Religious and political references avoided unless central to the brand | Risk of offence |
| Colours and symbols checked for regional meaning | Meanings vary |
| Names, honorifics and form fields suit Indian naming (single names, long names, no strict first/last split) | Form drop-off |
| Pronouns and greeting (e.g. use of "ji") fit the language and audience | Respect cues |

## Output

Cultural Fit score 0-100 per language/region version, with specific findings
and at most five fixes ranked by impact. Mark each finding as observed on the
site or a hypothesis to test.
